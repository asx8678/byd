**Why each is interesting to park**
1. **Smart:** 424 / 398 mm overhangs mean almost no tail swing, and the circle is 6.95 m kerb-to-kerb (rear engine, RWD). Rear sensors were a €300 option (ADAC); ams measured 7.2 / 7.1 m (left / right lock).
2. **208:** A baseline FWD supermini. The mirrors add 215 mm (1745 to 1960 mm), and a rear parking aid is standard on the Allure (ADAC).
3. **W124:** Lock angle, turns lock-to-lock and caster are all unverified; steering is power-assisted recirculating ball (auto-data.net). The only evidence of the big lock is indirect: steering stops sold "to limit the steering angle… and reduce the chance of a dragging tire on the inner wheel well" (https://rpm-depot.de/product/steering-stop-steering-angle-limiter-mercedes-190e-w201-w124-a1293300080/?lang=en), and a forum measurement of about 13 (unit not stated) camber change between full locks on a 500E (https://www.500eboard.co/forums/goto/post?id=53531).
4. **W223 L:** 5.29 m long with a ~1.2 m rear overhang (db). 10° rear steer takes the wall-to-wall circle from 39 ft (4.5°) down to 35.4 ft. A reversing camera is standard; PARKTRONIC and the 360° camera are listed as "Digital Extras" (MBUSA MY2027).
   - **How I derived the no-RAS figure:** MBUSA MY2022 says the 10° system cuts "up to 7 feet" (wording: "turning radius"), giving "less than 36 feet". Adding 7 ft to 35.4–36 ft gives ≈42.4–43 ft, i.e. ≈12.9–13.1 m wall-to-wall.
   - **Independent support:** ams says "unter elf Metern" with 10°, reduced "um bis zu zwei Meter".
5. **M3:** A 1903 mm body on 120–123 mm ground clearance (BMW); the front-lip height is not published. M xDrive adds 0.4 m of turning circle, and the overall steering ratio is 15.0:1.
6. **Ram:** 5.9 m × 2.08 m with a 14.08 m curb-to-curb circle and "Steering wheel turns 3.1" (Rebel 3.4). The air suspension's "Entry/Exit" mode drops it to 1926 mm.
7. **TX:** The front wheels turn to 63°, against about 38° for a normal car (https://www.topgear.com/car-reviews/ecity/first-drive), giving 8.45 m wall-to-wall on a 2.985 m wheelbase.
   - **TfL Conditions of Fitness:** Issue v9, 11 Dec 2017, read via https://web.archive.org/web/20190224115819/http://content.tfl.gov.uk/taxi-conditions-of-fitness-update.pdf; no later issue checked.
     - 7.1: U-turn on either lock without reversing "between two vertical parallel planes not more than 8.535 metres apart".
     - 7.2: "The wheel turning circle kerb to kerb on either lock must be not less than 7.62 metres in diameter" (sic).
     - 14.2: overall length must not exceed 5 m.
8. **Stretch limo:** The derived wheelbase is about 6 m, so the rear wheels cut far inside the front wheels' path. QVM allows at most a 120 in stretch and "nine plus a driver".
9. **Hummer EV:** 2.2 m wide (2.38 m with mirrors). CrabWalk moves it diagonally at low speed, and 4WS shrinks the circle from 13.5 m to 11.3 m. It has front and rear underbody cameras with "virtual spotter" views (GM).
10. **Sprinter:** 2.64 m tall, and the A4's rear overhang is 2021 mm. On the 2019 DE list, rear-door windows (F35), the reversing camera and the park packs were all options, so a base van has no rear glass.
11. **911:** Rear-engined, with a 1076 mm rear overhang and 122 mm maximum ground clearance (porsche.com). Rear-axle steering trims the circle from 11.2 m to 10.9 m.
12. **Octavia:**
    - **Braked towing (12%):** 1.0 TSI 1300 kg; 1.5 TSI 110 kW 1500 kg; 2.0 TDI 85 kW 1500 kg; 2.0 TDI 110 kW DSG 1600 kg.
    - **Unbraked:** 650–730 kg, below the trailer's 750 kg GVW. The trailer's brake status is unverified.
12b. **Trailer:** A ~1 m drawbar ahead of a 2.5 m box makes it react quickly when reversing. The overall width is 1760 mm against the 1300 mm box, so use 1760 for collision.
13. **Artic (96/53/EC Annex I, consolidated 14.08.2019, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:01996L0053-20190814):**
    - **Limits:** 16.50 m (1.1); 2.55 / 2.60 m (1.2); 4.00 m (1.3); swept ring R 12.50 / 5.30 m (1.5); kingpin to rear ≤12.00 m (1.6); kingpin to front ≤2.04 m (4.4).
    - **Geometry:** The axles sit about 7.6 m behind the kingpin, so expect big off-tracking. The fifth-wheel offset is unverified.
    - **Derived constraint:** tractor front to kingpin ≤16.50 − 12.00 = 4.50 m when the trailer uses the full 12.00 m.
14. **Citaro G:** 2.95 m across the mirrors, a 3.43 m rear overhang and front wheel lock of "53°/46°". EU 1.5a caps a bus's outswing at 0.60 m. It has Mercedes' ATC anti-jackknife system.

**Caveats and conflicts**
- **Turning-circle type:**
  - **Stated by the source:** smart (kerb and wall), Ram (curb-to-curb diameter), TX (wall), Hummer (curb-to-curb diameter), W223 (wall), Škoda (diameter).
  - **Sprinter:** Mercedes heads the columns "Wendekreis Ø / Spurkreis Ø". I mapped these to wall-to-wall / kerb-to-kerb by standard German usage. CarExpert NZ supports this: it lists wall-to-wall 15.7 / kerb-to-kerb 14.9 at a 3924 mm wheelbase, which matches the top of the price list's range for that row. The VA sheet calls 15.3 "kerb to kerb"; I followed Mercedes.
  - **All others:** type not stated; their size implies diameters.
- **smart turning circle:** 6.95 m (Daimler) vs 7.0 m (ADAC) vs 7.1 / 7.2 m measured (ams).
- **208 width:** 1745 mm (ADAC, ams) vs 1765 mm (autotijd).
- **W124:**
  - Height is 1431 / 1430 / 1428 mm depending on source.
  - cars-data says rear parking sensors were "Standard" on a 1993 W124, which looks auto-filled; AutoWiki shows none.
  - The 11.2 m turning circle comes from databases only.
- **W223:**
  - Width without mirrors is 76.9 in (MY2022) vs 75.6 in (MY2027).
  - The db's 12.5 m may be kerb-to-kerb, so it need not conflict with the ≈13 m wall-to-wall derived figure.
  - Wikipedia gives 13.1 / 12.2 / 11.2 m (none / 4.5° / 10°), citing ams, 17 May 2021, "Voll bis oben hin…", which I did not fetch; the variant is unknown. Its 13.1 m matches my derived no-RAS value, while its 12.2 and 11.2 m sit about 0.3–0.4 m above MBUSA's figures.
- **TX width:** 1945 mm (GSO certificate) vs 1874 mm (Wikipedia, uncited); LEVC only says "less than 2m".
- **Sprinter width:** 2019 mm (Mercedes BEG) vs 2020 mm (VA).
- **Octavia overhangs:** the db figures add up to 4690 mm, against Škoda's 4689 mm length.
- **Hummer:** GM's 2020 table carries a pre-production "estimates" disclaimer, but the 2025 gmc.com figures match it.
- **Krone trailer drawing:** a kingpin 1668 mm behind a square 2.55 m front would give a ≈2100 mm corner radius, over both the sheet's 2040 mm and the legal 2.04 m. Model chamfered or rounded front corners, or keep the kingpin ≤≈1592 mm behind the front (der.).
- **Citaro:** the brochure prints "2,500" for the G's BOKraft circle, evidently a typo for 25,000.

  - Track: 911, Sprinter, limo, Citaro.
  - Overhangs: W124, M3, Ram, TX, limo.
  - Actros: geometry beyond cab width and driven axle, including the fifth-wheel offset.
  - The limo's turning circle.
  - The trailer's hitch-to-axle distance.
