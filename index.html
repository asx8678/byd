Controls

Hold Forward or Reverse to move: a short press creeps at about 4 km/h, keep holding and the car accelerates up to 20 km/h (10 km/h in reverse), the button fills up as the speed stage rises; release and the car brakes. Drag the wheel round like a real steering wheel. The 360° panel at the top is rendered live from above the car, with the yellow guidelines bending with your steering like the car's own screen. Drag the 3D view to look around. Keyboard: ← → steer, ↑ ↓ drive, Space straighten, C view, X reset.

Driving model

Kinematic single-track model about the rear axle, which is accurate at parking speed: the car pivots around a point on the rear-axle line, the inner rear wheel cuts inside the front wheels, and the front corner swings wide when reversing. The front wheels follow Ackermann angles. A short press of Forward creeps at 4 km/h; after one second of holding the target speed climbs by 4 km/h every second up to 20 km/h (reverse 3 to 10 km/h), acceleration is limited to 2 m/s² and releasing brakes at 3.5 m/s², and the steering self-centres as the car rolls (switchable). The yellow guidelines show both leading corners; the thin cyan line is the end that swings: the inner rear wheel going forward, the outer front corner reversing.

Parking sensors

Twelve ultrasonic sensors as on the car: four in each bumper (two centre, two corner angled 35° out) and two per side. Each sees a 60° cone, 1.2 m ahead, 1.5 m behind, 0.6 m to the side, so a pillar beside a door is only heard once a corner sensor catches it, just like the real system. The beeper starts slow at the edge of range, speeds up through 1.0, 0.7 and 0.45 m, and becomes a continuous tone under 30 cm. Front sensors beep a higher note than the rear. Beeps need one tap on the wheel or a pedal first (phones only play sound after a touch). The sensor graphic at the right of the 3D view works like the car's own screen: the bands around the car light up from the outside in, the end that is listening is brighter, a STOP card appears under 30 cm in the direction of travel, and the edge of the view glows red on the side of the obstacle.

What counts as parked

All four corners inside the target bay lines, car stopped, heading within 6° of straight. Nose-in (as in the photos) or reversed both count; the result card shows your centring, angle and the gap to the bench.
